01

The mark

Two squares mid-step — one leading, one following, locked at the corner crossing. The outline mark is primary. The front square is the accent and sits at 90% opacity so the crossings visibly weave.

Construction

Each square's stroke is side ÷ 8. The front square offsets diagonally by side × 0.60 down-left, so each corner reaches 40% of the way into the other square. Clear space around the mark ≥ one stroke width on all sides. Minimum size: 20 px — below that, use the filled mark.

03

Wordmark & letter patterns

The wordmark is always lowercase, set in a geometric sans (Poppins / Montserrat class — rendered here as Avenir Next), weight 600, tracking −0.02em. The two-tone pattern is the brand's signature: the noun in Ink, the verb in Cobalt.

The pattern, generalized

Any compound name follows the same dance: base word in Ink, the moving part in Cobalt, never capitalized, one space or none. On teal surfaces the accent word takes Teal instead — the pattern survives the palette.

04

Color

The accent is Cobalt — a true blue. The violet-leaning periwinkle from early drafts is retired: hue sits at ~223°, never drifting past 230° into purple. Teal is the secondary family for illustration, charts, and alternate marks. Neutrals carry a slight blue bias.

05

On dark grounds

square dance

Ink is the ground, not a tint

On Ink, the back square turns white and Cobalt lightens two steps (#6E8FE0 / #8AA5EA) to hold contrast. Never place the standard cobalt mark on Ink.

06

Usage

doKeep the wordmark lowercase everywhere — window titles, docs, marketing.
doUse the filled mark below 20 px (favicons, list avatars).
doLet the crossing weave — keep the front square's 90% opacity.
don'tSlide back toward violet — if the blue reads purple, it's off-palette.
don'tRotate the mark, change the overlap, or outline the filled variant.
don'tMix the teal and cobalt families in one lockup.
don'tSet the wordmark in another face, capitalize it, or split the two-tone pattern.
